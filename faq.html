Registration

  • Is team participation allowed? If so, how many people can be on a team?+

    Yes. You may participate individually or as a team. There is no restriction on the maximum number of members in a team.

  • Is participation limited to college or graduate students?+

    This competition is designed to enhance the capabilities of undergraduate and graduate students, so participation is limited to those with student status. If you are currently enrolled as a student, even while also working (e.g., on a degree dispatch program), you are eligible to participate. However, if you have already graduated and are no longer a student, unfortunately, you would not be eligible.

Leaderboard

  • Can I change my leaderboard display name?+

    Yes. This field was added to the registration form after registration had already opened, so participants who registered earlier are currently shown as “Participant #XXX”. If you would like a different name, please email us at moa2026.competition@gmail.com and we will update it for you.
